Persian headings are a step smaller but get taller lines, because Persian letters reach further above and below the line than Latin ones.

Persian rules

  • The layout uses start and end instead of left and right, so the road, the cover spine, chevrons and the finish flag all mirror themselves.
  • Clocks, digits and the play button keep their direction. Only the layout flips.
  • Persian digits (۱۲۳), the ٪ sign, and dates in the Persian calendar: started ۱۷ مهر.
  • The week starts on Saturday (ش ی د س چ پ ج), so "this week" means the same thing it does in Iran.
  • No letter spacing, no italics and no capital-letter styling, since Persian letters must connect and the script has none of these.
  • Proper half-spaces (نیم‌فاصله): می‌خوانند، کتاب‌ها.
  • Book titles go in «guillemets» and are isolated, so a Latin title never scrambles the sentence around it.
  • Persian book covers have their binding on the right.
